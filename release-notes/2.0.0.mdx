* **ADDED** WebP for Quick Look now supports AVIF images, too! Animated AVIF images play at their original speed, just like animated WebP images.
  To preview AVIF files, enable the Quick Look extension in **System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Extensions → Quick Look**.
* **ADDED** Localized the app into 20 more languages. (Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Ukrainian, Vietnamese)
* **FIXED** Some WebP images were not played at the correct speed.

The free tier works the same way for both formats: static images are unlimited, and up to 10 animated WebP or AVIF files can be previewed per hour.
